# Your first paper: from materials to a complete draft

[中文课程入口](first-paper-course.md) · [English start](../START-HERE.en.md)

Use a fictional case before working on your own research, or bring your existing records. You do not need an English draft or a target journal to start. This course teaches the organization and expression of supplied evidence; subject-specific research decisions still need appropriate supervision.

## Start a session

```text
Read first-paper-course.en.md in the PAPER QUEST bundle.
I want to learn to write a complete paper from the beginning.
Use the fictional course case. Teach and give feedback in English.
Start with E01 in paper-workbook.en.md. Show only the current task
and any small example I need, then let me try before giving feedback.
If I get stuck, give one concrete hint. I may request an answer at any time.
Keep all fictional material separate from my real research.
```

For self-study, open the [bilingual evidence packet](paper-case-materials.md) and [English workbook](paper-workbook.en.md). To see the whole example first, open the [complete English model paper](paper-case-manuscript.md). The [stage-by-stage walkthrough](paper-case-walkthrough.md), [feedback instructions](paper-feedback.md), and [answer explanations](paper-answer-key.md) are primarily Chinese; an assistant can explain them in English. The learner workbook itself is available in both languages.

## What you will produce

| Task | What you do | Output |
|---|---|---|
| E01 | Separate known facts, unmeasured outcomes, and missing information | Evidence inventory |
| E02 | Limit a question to what the materials can answer | Question and outline |
| E03 | Explain material selection and counting rules | Methods paragraph |
| E04 | Calculate, select, and describe observations | Results table and paragraph |
| E05 | Compare two reading cards and introduce the task | Introduction with attribution |
| E06 | Answer the question and discuss scope | Discussion and conclusion |
| E07 | Condense the completed paper | Title, abstract, keywords |
| E08 | Assemble and check the same claims across sections | Complete practice manuscript |
| T01 | Work with different materials and less support | Transfer exercise and feedback |

Here, drafting starts with methods and results. The finished paper uses title, abstract, introduction, methods, results, discussion, conclusion, and references. This is one descriptive document-comparison genre, not a universal chapter structure. Other genres can use the [section handbook](section-handbook.md).

## How feedback works

Try a small task, receive feedback tied to a specific sentence and source, revise, then compare the two versions. You can write in notes, Chinese, or English; language proficiency and understanding of the evidence are checked separately. Ask for a model answer whenever helpful. Direct editing remains available and does not require completing exercises.

If evidence is missing, the assistant should say which record or table to find, where to look, what to ask your supervisor, and what can safely be written now. The [missing-materials guide](missing-materials.md) contains these bridges. Fictional case data must never be relabelled as your own findings.

## What completion means

Reading an answer, revising the same task, and completing a new task without hints are different observations. Feedback should name what you independently did in that task and what still needed support. E08 produces a complete fictional teaching paper; it is not a submission-ready study. T01 tests transfer within this course, not general research competence. No student learning-outcome study has been conducted for this bundle.

To continue later, request a [continuation card](../assets/learning-checkpoint.md) with your task ID, draft version, hints used, unresolved questions, and next action. Saving requires your request; cross-session memory is not assumed.
