# 农业与兽医用词

使用条件及依据标记见 [选词说明](../usage-selection.md)。例句均为原创语言示例；术语仍需匹配实际测量和管理对象。

<a id="a01"></a>
## a01 农学与园艺

写作关注：交代作物、管理动作、产量构成和生育阶段，保持物种名称一致。

| 候选词 | 中文意图 | 常见搭配 | 选择条件 | 自拟例句 | 替换边界 | 依据 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| <a id="u-a01-01"></a>yield / biomass / productivity | 产量、生物量、生产力 | grain yield; aboveground biomass; crop productivity | 按收获对象、质量定义及时间基准选择。 | Grain yield was reported separately from total biomass. | 生物量增加不自动代表经济产量增加。 | E |
| <a id="u-a01-02"></a>apply / amend / incorporate | 施用、改良、混入 | apply fertilizer; amend soil; incorporate residues | apply 指施用；amend 需指定改良材料；incorporate 指混入。 | Residues were incorporated into the soil. | 表面覆盖不能替换为 incorporate。 | E |
| <a id="u-a01-03"></a>cultivar / variety / genotype | 栽培品种、变种、基因型 | compare cultivars; identify a botanical variety; evaluate genotypes | 依据育种、分类或遗传层级选择。 | The experiment compared the selected cultivars. | 不将品种名直接改成物种名；分类格式另查期刊。 | S08 |
| <a id="u-a01-04"></a>establish / emerge / germinate | 建植、出苗、萌发 | establish a crop; seedling emergence; seed germination | germinate、emerge、establish 描述不同阶段。 | Seedling emergence was recorded separately from germination. | 萌发成功不直接代表田间建植成功。 | E |

<a id="a02"></a>
## a02 土壤与土地管理

写作关注：保留库量、速率、过程、指标及处理方式的单位和边界。

| 候选词 | 中文意图 | 常见搭配 | 选择条件 | 自拟例句 | 替换边界 | 依据 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| <a id="u-a02-01"></a>sequester / store / retain | 固存、储存、保留 | sequester carbon; store carbon; retain nutrients | sequester 需明确新增与时间基准；store 描述库存；retain 关注保留。 | Soil carbon stocks were measured at the two sampling dates. | 较高库存不单独证明处理期间新增固存。 | E |
| <a id="u-a02-02"></a>mineralize / decompose / respire | 矿化、分解、呼吸 | mineralize organic carbon; decompose litter; soil respiration | 过程范围及被测组分不同，按方法定义使用。 | The study measured carbon dioxide release during incubation. | 只有气体读数时，不能补写所有分解去向。 | E |
| <a id="u-a02-03"></a>sorb / adsorb / absorb | 吸着、吸附、吸收 | sorb to soil constituents; adsorb onto a surface; absorb into a phase | sorption 可包含不同机制；adsorption 指界面富集。 | The experiment quantified sorption without separating its mechanisms. | 未区分机制时，避免将 sorption 全部替换成 adsorption。 | S03 |
| <a id="u-a02-04"></a>aggregate / stabilize / occlude | 团聚、稳定、包埋 | aggregate formation; stabilize organic matter; occlude particles | 按结构形成、状态变化及物理位置区分。 | Organic matter was measured within the aggregate fraction. | 位于团聚体内不单独证明长期稳定。 | E |

<a id="a03"></a>
## a03 林学

写作关注：林分尺度、恢复目标、自然更新和经营措施分别表述。

| 候选词 | 中文意图 | 常见搭配 | 选择条件 | 自拟例句 | 替换边界 | 依据 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| <a id="u-a03-01"></a>restore / reforest / regenerate | 恢复、重新造林、更新 | restore a forest ecosystem; reforest land; natural regeneration | restore 指生态恢复；reforest 指恢复林木覆盖；regenerate 关注新个体补充。 | The project monitored natural regeneration after disturbance. | 种树面积不能直接写成生态系统恢复面积。 | S04 |
| <a id="u-a03-02"></a>thin / harvest / clear | 间伐、采伐、清除 | thin a stand; harvest timber; clear vegetation | 按操作范围和对象使用。 | The management treatment involved stand thinning. | 间伐不能改写为完全清除林分。 | E |
| <a id="u-a03-03"></a>recruitment / establishment / survival | 补充、定居、存活 | tree recruitment; seedling establishment; seedling survival | 依样方复查及阶段定义选择。 | Seedling survival was assessed between consecutive surveys. | 存活个体数不直接代表新补充个体数。 | E |
| <a id="u-a03-04"></a>stand / canopy / forest | 林分、冠层、森林 | stand structure; canopy cover; forest extent | 结构单元、垂直层和景观范围不同。 | Canopy cover was measured within each stand. | 不用 forest 泛指所有冠层测量对象。 | E |

<a id="a04"></a>
## a04 渔业与水产

写作关注：区分资源量、捕捞量、补充量和养殖生产指标。

| 候选词 | 中文意图 | 常见搭配 | 选择条件 | 自拟例句 | 替换边界 | 依据 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| <a id="u-a04-01"></a>rebuild / restore / replenish | 资源重建、恢复、补充 | rebuild a fish stock; restore habitat; replenish a population | rebuild 可用于达到资源量管理目标；restore 依生态对象；replenish 指补充。 | The assessment examined progress toward rebuilding the stock. | rebuild 是此语境的正式搭配，不能据词面统一换为 restructure。 | S01 |
| <a id="u-a04-02"></a>catch / landings / stock | 渔获、登陆量、资源量 | total catch; reported landings; spawning stock biomass | 三者所含对象与统计范围不同。 | Reported landings were analyzed separately from stock estimates. | 未登陆的渔获不能默认为已包含在 landings 内。 | E |
| <a id="u-a04-03"></a>recruitment / stocking / release | 补充量、放养、放流 | recruitment to a stock; stocking a pond; release of juveniles | 自然或总体补充与管理投放动作分别表达。 | The report distinguished recruitment estimates from stocking records. | 投放数不直接等于最终补充量。 | E |
| <a id="u-a04-04"></a>feed intake / feed conversion / growth | 采食、饲料转化、生长 | measure feed intake; estimate feed conversion ratio; assess growth | 按各指标定义与单位报告。 | Growth and feed conversion were evaluated separately. | 生长快不自动等于饲料转化率高；比率方向需核对。 | E |

<a id="a05"></a>
## a05 动物科学与兽医

写作关注：明确动物层级、群体、健康终点和生产终点，避免用人类试验词套用。

| 候选词 | 中文意图 | 常见搭配 | 选择条件 | 自拟例句 | 替换边界 | 依据 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| <a id="u-a05-01"></a>herd / flock / cohort | 畜群、禽群、队列 | herd health; flock management; study cohort | 物种群体与研究抽样群体分别命名。 | The cohort included animals from several herds. | 一个 cohort 可跨多个 herd，不能互换计数单位。 | E |
| <a id="u-a05-02"></a>weight gain / growth rate / body condition | 增重、生长速率、体况 | daily weight gain; growth rate; body condition score | 质量变化、时间标准化变化与评分是不同指标。 | Body condition was scored independently of weight gain. | 体况评分不能改成生长速率。 | E |
| <a id="u-a05-03"></a>welfare / health / performance | 福利、健康、生产表现 | assess welfare; monitor health; evaluate performance | 分别依行为、生理、疾病或生产指标定义。 | The study assessed welfare and production performance separately. | 生产表现改善不足以替代福利评价。 | E |
| <a id="u-a05-04"></a>treat / manage / prevent | 治疗、管理、预防 | treat a condition; manage a herd; prevent disease | 实际干预目的与证据范围不同。 | The report described herd management practices. | 管理措施的实施不能直接写成已证明预防疾病。 | E |
