# 参考答案与核对依据 / Reference answers

**虚构教学材料，勿作为真实研究引用。Fictional teaching material, not real research.**

[中文练习册](paper-workbook.zh-CN.md) · [English workbook](paper-workbook.en.md) · [反馈流程](paper-feedback.md)

解释主要为中文，示范段为英文；可请助手逐项用英语讲解。答案用于核对事实、范围和章节任务，语言表达可以不同。主动打开答案即表示选择看示范，随后同题练习不要标记为无提示完成。

## E01：事实清单

对象是服务说明卡，六对、十二份文档；计数对象是每个版本的编号条目和 Contact 行。没有读者人数、耗时、理解成绩或服务表现记录。P01 提供材料范围，P02 提供定义。

> The packet contains six pairs of service cards, twelve documents in total. Numbered items and contact-line presence can be recorded from the text. Reader comprehension and access to help were not measured.

“更好理解”需要读者理解方面的证据；编号条目可以据原文核查；有 Contact 不能证明获得帮助。无需把这些边界进一步写成对真实研究的缺陷指控。

## E02：问题与提纲

> How do numbered-item counts and contact-line presence differ between the earlier and later versions of the six supplied service cards?

引言说明问题和适用范围；方法说明材料及定义；结果分别回答两项比较；讨论说明有依据的解释和仍未测量的范围。各章名称可以依实际体裁调整。

## E03：方法段示范

> We used all six matched pairs of fictional service cards supplied in the packet, with one earlier and one later document per pair. We counted top-level numbered items in each document, counting an item once even when it contained several actions. Titles and contact lines were excluded. Contact-line presence was recorded as 1 for a separate line beginning with “Contact:” and as 0 otherwise. We summarized numbered-item totals, means per card, and paired changes, using the earlier total as the denominator for relative change. Contact counts were summarized separately. No reader observations, independent coding-reliability assessment, or inferential tests were supplied or performed in this exercise.

C01-B 有三项、Contact 记 1。第 2 项包含两个动作但只有一个顶层编号。方法记录描述本练习，不能迁移成其他稿件已经执行过的步骤。

## E04：结果核对

| Card | Earlier items | Later items | Earlier contact | Later contact | Item change |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| C01 | 4 | 3 | 0 | 1 | -1 |
| C02 | 5 | 4 | 1 | 1 | -1 |
| C03 | 4 | 3 | 0 | 0 | -1 |
| C04 | 6 | 4 | 0 | 1 | -2 |
| C05 | 5 | 4 | 1 | 1 | -1 |
| C06 | 6 | 3 | 0 | 1 | -3 |

总数 30、21；每卡均值 5.0、3.5；减少 9，9/30 = 30%。逐对均减少，范围 1 至 3。Contact 为 2/6 与 5/6，相差 3/6，即 50 个百分点；若算相对增加则是 (5−2)/2 = 150%，两者不能互换。正文直接报告 2 与 5 已经清楚。

> The total number of numbered items decreased from 30 to 21, corresponding to means of 5.0 and 3.5 per card and a 30% reduction relative to the earlier total. Every later card had fewer numbered items, with reductions ranging from one to three. Contact lines appeared on two earlier cards and five later cards.

不能写 `tasks` 替代 `numbered items`，不能添加统计显著性，不能把 Contact 变化说成支持服务改善。

## E05：引言核对

L1 支持编号条目与动作/读者表现的区别。L2 支持 Contact 存在与帮助可得性的区别。二者共同限定文档特征可以怎样解释，未提供真实效果研究。

一版完整引言见[范文 §1](paper-case-manuscript.md)。也可用更短结构：先交代文档中的两个特征，比较 L1/L2，再落到六对卡片。不同顺序可以成立，只要读者能理解本文任务及其依据。省略夸大的领域空白与无依据的 `many studies`。

## E06：讨论与结论核对

> The lower item counts describe a change in document organization. In C01, choosing a room and a time slot was grouped under one later number, while C06 combined going to the desk and requesting a receipt. These examples support an interpretation about grouping, but do not establish fewer underlying actions or faster completion. Contact lines were more frequent in the later documents; their presence does not establish the availability of assistance. The comparison is limited to the six supplied pairs, with no reader outcomes or independent coding-reliability assessment available.

可用结论：

> The later versions of the six fictional cards contained fewer numbered items and more often included a contact line. These findings describe the supplied documents and leave reader and service outcomes unmeasured.

重点是结果、原文例子和解释之间的对应。不要把“不知道效果”写成“已经证明无效果”。

## E07：标题摘要核对

标题可用 *Numbered items and contact lines in two versions of six service cards*。摘要见[完整稿](paper-case-manuscript.md)。不要求照抄，但应保留六对、两个指标、主要数字及文档范围。`novel method`、效率改善、真实参与者均无材料支持。关键词可以用 document comparison、service instructions、contact information 等确实涉及的概念。

## T01：新材料答案

| Notice | Earlier items | Later items | Earlier contact | Later contact | Item change |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| N01 | 2 | 3 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| N02 | 4 | 4 | 1 | 0 | 0 |
| N03 | 3 | 2 | 0 | 0 | -1 |
| N04 | 5 | 3 | 1 | 1 | -2 |

总数 14→12；每通知均值 3.5→3.0；总数减少 2，2/14 ≈ 14.3%。两对减少、一对增加、一对不变。Contact 为 3→2，方向与原案例相反。没有原文，无法判断动作是否合并或计数是否准确反映原文。

**Question:** How do recorded numbered-item counts and contact-line presence differ between the two versions of the four supplied event notices?

**Materials/methods:**

> We used the supplied count records for four matched pairs of fictional event notices. The original texts were unavailable, so we summarized the recorded values using the supplied definitions without independently recounting the documents.

**Results:**

> Recorded numbered-item totals decreased from 14 to 12, with means of 3.5 and 3.0 per notice. This is a 14.3% reduction relative to the earlier total. Two later notices contained fewer recorded items, one contained more, and one was unchanged. Contact lines were recorded for three earlier notices and two later notices.

**Discussion:**

> The aggregate decline in recorded item counts was not shared by every pair. Contact-line presence also decreased, so the two features cannot be described as jointly improving. Without the original notices, we cannot check the recorded counts or determine whether actions were combined under fewer numbers. The records support a description of this collection, with reader outcomes and explanations for the textual changes remaining unavailable.

**Four-sentence abstract:**

> This fictional exercise compared recorded numbered-item counts and contact-line presence in four paired event notices. We summarized supplied count records without access to the original texts. Total counts decreased from 14 to 12, although pairwise changes were mixed, and contact-line counts decreased from three to two. These records describe the supplied collection but cannot establish how text was regrouped or how readers performed.

反馈应优先检查学生是否机械照搬“全部减少”“联系方式增加”和“动作合并”。语言通顺仍可能包含这些材料错误。具体写法可不同；未报告百分比也可正确完成摘要。
