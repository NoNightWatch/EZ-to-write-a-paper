# 第一次试用 / First-session demo

这是原创虚构教学材料，无真实受试者或研究。只用于了解交付格式，不可作为研究证据。预期输出是示范，实际模型表述可以不同。

## 中文请求，英文稿件

把以下整段发给能读取完整技能文件夹的助手：

```text
请使用 academic-manuscript-polish，只修改下面虚构稿件的语言。
保留数字和结论范围，英文修订，中文解释。
先给干净稿，再讲两处改动。暂不保存文件。

We interviewed 18 students about feedback timing.
12 of them said it was too late. We wanted to know their views.
```

## English request

```text
Use academic-manuscript-polish to edit only the language of this
fictional paragraph. Keep its numbers and scope. Give a clean
revision, then explain two edits in English. Do not save files.

We interviewed 18 students about feedback timing.
12 of them said it was too late. We wanted to know their views.
```

## 一种可用输出 / One acceptable result

> We interviewed 18 students about their views on feedback timing. Twelve participants reported that the feedback arrived too late.

- 合并重复的目的表达，保留访谈对象与主题。 / Combine the repeated purpose while retaining the participants and topic.
- 明确 it 指反馈，并用 reported 保留“受访者陈述”的身份。 / Clarify what “it” refers to and retain the attribution to participants through “reported.”

数字 18 和 12 的含义保持。不得增加成绩、因果解释、显著性、代表性或真实引用。 / Preserve the meanings of 18 and 12. Add no claims about grades, causation, significance, representativeness, or real citations.

## 再试一次局部控制 / Try a local control

> 第一句恢复原句，保留第二句修改，解释只用一句话。

> Restore the original first sentence. Keep the revised second sentence and explain in one sentence.

可用结果应恢复第一句为 `We interviewed 18 students about feedback timing.`，同时保留第二句。若原稿不可访问，助手应先请求原句。 / A valid result restores the original first sentence while keeping the revised second sentence. If the original is inaccessible, the assistant should request it.
